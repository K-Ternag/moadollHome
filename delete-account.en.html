01MoaDoll works without an account

MoaDoll (com.moadoll) does not create a separate MoaDoll account or server profile. Google account authorization is used for optional Drive backup and restoration; Google Play purchase records are managed separately. This page explains deletion of device data, images, file and Drive backups, purchase records, and support correspondence. This website cannot remotely erase data on your device or in external services.

02Delete individual dolls and cards

Select a doll in My dolls and use its delete action. Delete saved photocards from the studio’s load list. Deleting one item may leave editing drafts, dolls in other cards, and separately exported PNG images.

03Clear all app data on your device

Open Android Settings → Apps → MoaDoll → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear data (or Clear storage). This resets app-private dolls, cards, drafts, owned items, coins, reward records, local purchase verification information, settings, the selected city, and stored search and weather data. Clearing cache alone does not remove all app data. The one-hour weather and 24-hour city-search periods govern cache reuse, not automatic deletion.

Menu names may differ by device. Restoring deleted editing data requires an existing backup. Manual backups exclude finished PNGs, coin balances, and All Access purchase information. Clearing app data does not delete external backups or Google Play purchase records.

04Delete gallery images and shared copies

Use your gallery or file manager to delete PNGs in the MoaDoll album or Pictures/MoaDoll, and empty the trash if needed. Manage copies saved elsewhere or in photo backup services separately. Delete images shared through messaging or social services there. The developer cannot delete copies saved by recipients.

05Review system backups

Check Android backup settings and your connected Google or manufacturer backup service. Manage or delete backups using the provider’s available controls. Uninstalling or clearing app data does not instantly erase existing cloud backups. Old data may be restored during reinstallation or device transfer, so also check restore settings.

06Request deletion of support correspondence

Email the published support address from the address you used for your original request, with the subject “Delete MoaDoll support correspondence.” Include only the minimum information needed to locate it, such as the date and subject. You do not need to attach your dolls or card files. The scope and outcome will be communicated by email.

Support emails and attachments: retained for 30 days after the request is resolved, then deleted. At the end of the retention period or when fulfilling a verified deletion request, the operator permanently deletes support emails and attachments from the mailbox and trash and deletes attachment copies saved separately for troubleshooting. The email provider’s system records and backups follow its own policies.

07Delete file backups

Find and delete the MoaDoll backup files you saved using your file manager, and empty the trash where needed. Delete copies on other devices or cloud services separately. Uninstalling or clearing app data does not delete all backup files you saved.

08Delete Google Drive backups and disconnect access

On a computer, sign in to Google Drive with the account used for backup, then open Settings → Manage apps → MoaDoll → Options → Delete hidden app data. Menu names and availability may vary. App-specific backups are separate from the ordinary Drive file list and Android device backups. Include older files left behind by failed cleanup.

You can revoke MoaDoll’s access through your Google Account’s third-party connections controls. Disconnecting revokes access, so check deletion of existing backups separately. Uninstalling the app from your device does not delete all Drive backups. See Google’s linked guidance below.

09Google Play purchase records

Clearing app data removes local purchase verification information but does not delete Google Play transaction records, cancel the purchase, or issue a refund. Review transactions under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history in Google Play or in the Google payments center. Use Google’s privacy controls or support for record management and deletion requests. Google may retain records needed for legal or accounting purposes; the MoaDoll developer cannot directly delete them. You can restore All Access using the Google Play account that made the purchase.

10Information held by advertising, weather, and other services

Where the app provides advertising privacy options, you can manage consent there. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Changing these settings does not mean all previously processed records are deleted. Consult the privacy policies of Google AdMob, WeatherAPI (Zoomash Ltd), GitHub, and other relevant providers for retention and deletion requests. Delete local weather data using the Clear all app data steps above.
