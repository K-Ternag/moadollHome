01Scope

This policy covers MoaDoll (Android package com.moadoll) and its introduction website. The current app has no login, account creation, advertising, in-app purchases, developer-server uploads, or analytics SDKs. Local storage, Android backup, user-initiated sharing, website visits, and support correspondence are described separately below.

02Data processed on your device

Doll names, appearance and outfits, editing drafts, card layouts, backgrounds, frames, captions, stickers, speech bubbles, save times, language, and tutorial settings support editing and resuming your work. Inventory, points, and gift history from earlier versions may also remain. This data is kept in the app’s local database and settings storage and is not sent to a developer server. Personal information you type into a card may also appear in its exported image.

03Saving and sharing images

When you choose to save an image, the app writes a PNG to locations such as Pictures/MoaDoll and may create temporary working files. When you share, the app temporarily grants image read access to the app you select in the Android share sheet. Images sent to that service and copies kept by recipients are subject to that service’s policies. Friendship cards work the same way and do not create friend lists or invitation tracking data.

04Android backup and device transfer

The app permits Android automatic backup and device transfer, including its database and settings. Depending on your device, operating system, and backup settings, system backup providers such as Google may process this data or restore it to another device. This is separate from developer-server synchronization. Manage backup and deletion with your device and backup provider. Clearing app data does not guarantee immediate deletion of an existing backup.

05Permissions and access

On Android 8 and 9, the app may request write access to shared storage to save images. Android 10 and later use the system media storage API. The current app does not declare internet, advertising ID, location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. Refusing an optional permission may limit the related saving feature. You can change permissions in Android settings.

06Website visits and support

This website does not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, accounts, or a server that collects support messages. GitHub Pages may record visitor IP addresses for security. Opening Google Play or sending email is subject to the relevant service’s policies. If you email the published support address, your sender address, any name you provide, message, and attachments are used to respond and resolve your request. Please only provide necessary information.

07Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app or clear app data in Android settings. Deleting one item does not remove information in other drafts or cards, old scenes, temporary files, separately exported PNGs, or backups. See Delete your data for full reset instructions. Support correspondence retention: To be confirmed before release. System backups, shared copies, and GitHub access logs follow the respective providers’ policies and your settings.

08Control and protection

Editable content is managed in app-private storage. Exported images and shared copies can remain outside that storage. You can view, edit, and delete dolls and cards in the app, and request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of support correspondence through the published contact address. Minimum necessary identity verification may be requested. The developer cannot remotely erase your device or external shared copies.

09Children and policy changes

The current app does not ask for age, account details, or contact information. Guardians should help children avoid putting names, schools, contact details, or other personal information into cards or support messages. Guardians can contact support to request deletion of personal information a child submitted in correspondence. Changes to data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date and additional notice where needed. Refer to the release store listing for the intended age range and available regions.
