01Scope

This policy covers MoaDoll (Android package com.moadoll) and its introduction website. You can use MoaDoll without creating a separate MoaDoll account. Basic editing data is stored on your device. Ads use Google AdMob, All Access purchases use Google Play Billing, optional backup and restoration use Google Drive, and weather uses WeatherAPI. When you use these features, the information described below may be sent to the relevant service. Information processed by external services and SDKs is covered by this policy even without a separate developer server.

02Data processed on your device

Doll names, appearance and outfits, editing drafts, photocard layouts, backgrounds, frames, captions, stickers, speech bubbles, save times, language, and tutorial settings support editing and resuming your work. The age range used for advertising settings is stored on your device; the app does not collect your date of birth. Owned items, coin balances, reward records, purchase verification information, ad frequency and diagnostic records, the selected city, and search and weather results may also be stored locally to provide features. Records from earlier versions may remain.

Basic editing data is kept in the app’s local database and settings storage. Personal information you type into a card may appear in exported images and optional backups. See the sections below for backup and external-service processing.

03Saving and sharing images

When you choose to save an image, the app writes a PNG to locations such as Pictures/MoaDoll and may create temporary working files. When you share, the app temporarily grants image read access to the app you select in the Android share sheet. Images sent to that service and copies kept by recipients are subject to that service’s policies. Friendship cards work the same way and do not create friend lists or invitation tracking data.

04File, Google Drive, and system backups

When you choose to create a backup, it includes dolls, photocards, written text, owned items, and that day’s reward records. You can save a backup file to a location you choose or, after Google account authorization, upload it to app-specific storage in your Drive. Manual backups exclude finished PNGs, coin balances, and All Access purchase information. Keep PNGs separately and use Google Play purchase restoration for All Access.

Google Drive access is limited to app-specific data and does not grant access to all ordinary Drive files. The access token received during authorization is used for Drive backup and restoration requests. After an upload succeeds and is verified, the app removes older backups to keep the latest one. Older files may remain if cleanup fails. App-specific backups do not appear in the ordinary Drive file list.

Separately, the app permits Android automatic backup and device transfer, covering databases and settings included in its backup rules. Depending on your device, operating system, and backup settings, system backup providers such as Google may process this data or restore it to another device. Manual-backup exclusions do not mean the same exclusions apply to system backups. Manage system backup and deletion through your device and backup provider.

05Permissions and access

The production build includes permissions for internet access and network status, Google Play Billing, advertising ID, and advertising services. On Android 8 and 9, the app may request write access to shared storage to save images. Android 10 and later use the system media storage API.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions and does not read the device’s GPS location. Refusing an optional permission may limit the related saving feature. You can change permissions in Android settings. Google Drive access occurs when you choose to authorize access with your Google account.

06Website visits and support

This website does not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, accounts, or a server that collects support messages. GitHub Pages may record visitor IP addresses for security. Opening Google Play or sending email is subject to the relevant service’s policies. If you email the published support address, your sender address, any name you provide, message, and attachments are used to respond and resolve your request. Please only provide necessary information.

07Retention and deletion

Device data: Manage dolls, cards, settings, local purchase verification information, and stored weather data through in-app deletion or Android’s app data controls. Use Clear data / Clear storage to reset all local app data. Clear cache alone does not remove everything. The one-hour weather and 24-hour city-search periods govern cache reuse; they are not retention periods after which data is automatically deleted.

Saved images and file backups: Delete them through your gallery or file manager, including trash and separate copies where needed. Uninstalling the app does not remove all PNGs or backup files in shared storage. Copies held by recipients or external services require separate management.

Drive backups: Older backups are removed after a new backup is verified, but cleanup failures can leave files behind. To delete all app-specific backup data, delete MoaDoll’s hidden app data through Manage apps in Google Drive settings. Manage Android system backups separately through your device and backup provider. Uninstalling or disconnecting Google does not mean all external backups are deleted.

Google Play purchase records: Local purchase verification information and Google’s transaction records are separate. Clearing app data, uninstalling, or disconnecting Google does not delete Google Play purchase records or issue a refund. Available controls and deletion options follow Google Play and Google payments settings, support procedures, and policies.

Support emails and attachments: retained for 30 days after the request is resolved, then deleted. At the end of the retention period or when fulfilling a verified deletion request, the operator permanently deletes support emails and attachments from the mailbox and trash and deletes attachment copies saved separately for troubleshooting. The email provider’s system records and backups follow its own policies.

External-service retention and deletion, including advertising, weather, and GitHub access records, follow the providers’ policies and settings. See Delete your data for detailed steps and links.

08Control and protection

Editable content is managed in app-private storage. Exported images and shared copies can remain outside that storage. You can view, edit, and delete dolls and cards in the app, and request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of support correspondence through the published contact address. Minimum necessary identity verification may be requested. The developer cannot remotely erase your device or external shared copies.

09Children and policy changes

Intended audience: All ages, including children.

MoaDoll stores only an age range on your device for advertising settings and does not collect your date of birth. Before you make an age selection, the app does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads. For users under 18 and users who do not specify an age range, the app applies child-protection settings configured to block personalized advertising, remarketing, and transmission of the advertising ID. Adults receive ads according to the applicable consent procedures and settings. You can change your age range in the app settings.

Guardians should help children avoid putting names, schools, contact details, or other personal information into cards or support messages. Guardians can contact support to request deletion of personal information a child submitted in correspondence. Changes to data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date and additional notice where needed.

10Advertising and related information

The app provides banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. By default, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, advertising IDs, app set IDs and, where applicable, identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Interactions include app launches, taps, and video views. Diagnostics include app startup time, hang rate, and energy use. An IP address may be used to estimate approximate location. Actual processing depends on age-related advertising settings and consent status. Before an age selection is made, the app does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads. For users under 18 and users who do not specify an age range, the app is configured to block personalized advertising, remarketing, and transmission of the advertising ID.

Google consent flows and privacy options are provided depending on the applicable region and settings. Where required, you can manage advertising consent through the app’s privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. While All Access is active, the app does not request or display ads. See Google’s official SDK disclosure below for processing details and differences by configuration.

11Google Play Billing

Google Play processes All Access payments. The app processes product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status, and signed purchase information to verify purchases, grant access, and restore purchases, and stores some of this information on your device. Information needed for purchase confirmation is processed through Google Play Billing. The app does not directly collect or store card numbers or bank account information. Google’s policies apply to payment and transaction information it processes.

12WeatherAPI weather features

For weather requests, the app sends the coordinates of the default city or a city you select to WeatherAPI. City searches send the search text you enter, and the provider may process your connection IP address during communication. The app does not request location permission or read the device’s GPS location. The selected city and search and weather results are stored on your device to reduce requests and provide the feature. The MoaDoll operator does not retain these weather request data on a separate developer server. A selected city’s coordinates do not necessarily represent your actual current location.

13External services and international processing

Google: Provides AdMob advertising and consent management, Google Play Billing, optional Google Drive backup and restoration, Android system backup, and Gmail for receiving support messages. Relevant information is transmitted when each service is used. Google processes information on servers worldwide. Its published data center locations include the United States, Ireland, Finland, and Singapore. These are infrastructure locations, not a statement that every user's data is stored in all of those countries.

Google's retention periods depend on the data, settings, and purpose. Account content may remain until deleted; transaction, security, and legally required records may be retained longer. Google's general deletion process usually takes about two months, and encrypted backups may retain data for up to six months. Exceptions can delay deletion or require continued retention. See Delete your data for Drive backups and purchase records.

Zoomash Ltd (WeatherAPI, United Kingdom): May process city coordinates, search text, and connection IP addresses during weather and city search requests. Its public privacy policy lists servers in the United Kingdom and Finland. Information may be processed outside your country when you use the service. WeatherAPI processes, retains, and deletes API data under its privacy policy and terms of service. Public information does not establish the specific retention period applicable to each MoaDoll API request, so this policy does not state a fixed period. For processing details or deletion requests, contact MoaDoll support or WeatherAPI at support@weatherapi.com.

GitHub: GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries where its affiliates or subprocessors operate. Retention depends on purpose, contractual obligations, legal requirements, and dispute resolution. Its public policy does not give a single fixed retention period for Pages visitor logs. Privacy requests can be sent to privacy@github.com.

Support email is processed by Gmail and the sender's email service. The operator retains and deletes support emails and attachments as described above; provider system records and backups follow the respective provider's policies.
